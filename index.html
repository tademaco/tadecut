You film wide, from the water, the air or the road. Your audience scrolls tall. TadeCut bridges the two without a full video editor.

Cut fast

Split at the playhead, trim the ends, drag clips into order. Duplicate a clip to show the same moment twice with a different crop. Undo anything.

How it works

Three steps

  1. Drop in your videos

    MP4 or MOV from your phone, drone or action cam, 4K HEVC included. Add one clip or a whole session.

  2. Cut and frame

    Press S to split, drag to trim. Move the vertical frame where the action is, or let it follow with keyframes.

  3. Add music and export

    Pick a track, press ⌘E. Your vertical video is ready to post on Instagram, TikTok or YouTube.

FAQ

Which videos can I use?

Any normal Mac video: MP4 and MOV, including 4K HEVC from drones, phones and action cams.

What do I get out?

A 1080p MP4 (H.264 video, AAC audio) in 9:16, 4:5 or 1:1. It uploads straight to Instagram, TikTok and YouTube Shorts.

Can the frame follow the action?

Yes. Switch a clip to Moving, set a few keyframes, and the frame glides smoothly between them, panning and zooming as you set it.

Can I add music?

Yes, one music track per project. You set the volume and where in the song it starts, and it fades out at the end. Make sure you have the rights to the music you use.

Is it a subscription?

No. €8.99 once on the Mac App Store, with no in-app purchases.

Do I need an account or internet?

No. TadeCut works fully offline and has no sign-in. Your videos never leave your Mac.

Which Macs does it run on?

Any Mac with macOS 14 Sonoma or later, Apple silicon or Intel.

Is there an iPhone or Windows version?

No, TadeCut is a Mac app.
