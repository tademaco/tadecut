2. The App collects no data

The App processes your videos, music and project files only locally, on your Mac. Specifically:

  • No files you import or export are uploaded or sent anywhere.
  • There is no account, registration or sign-in.
  • There are no analytics, usage statistics, crash-reporting SDKs, advertising or tracking technologies of any kind in the App.
  • The App does not need an internet connection to work and does not contact our servers.

This is why the App’s App Store privacy label says “Data Not Collected”.

Files on your Mac

Projects (.tadecut files) and autosaves are stored on your Mac only. They contain your edit decisions and references to the location of your source files. You can delete them at any time. We have no access to them.

Crash reports from Apple

If you have chosen in macOS settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may provide us with anonymised crash reports and diagnostics. This is controlled by you in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements and is processed under Apple’s privacy policy. We use such reports only to fix bugs.

3. Purchases through Apple

TadeCut is sold through the Mac App Store. Apple handles the purchase, payment, downloads, refunds and any related personal data as an independent controller under its own privacy policy (apple.com/legal/privacy). We do not receive your name, payment details or Apple ID from Apple. We only receive aggregated, non-identifying sales reports.

4. The Website

The Website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking, and does not include third-party tracking scripts.

To display the pages, the Website loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google servers and your IP address is transmitted to Google. Like any web server, our hosting provider may briefly record technical access data (such as IP address, date and time, and requested page) in server logs, solely to deliver the Website and keep it secure (legal basis: our legitimate interest, Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). These logs are not used to identify you and are deleted after a short period.

5. When you email us

If you contact us at SUPPORT_EMAIL, we use your email address and the content of your message only to answer you (legal basis: Article 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR). We keep this correspondence only as long as needed to help you and to meet legal record-keeping obligations, and we do not share it with third parties except our email provider, who processes it on our behalf.

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectify and erase your personal data, to restrict or object to processing, and to data portability. Because the App collects no data, in practice this only concerns emails you sent us. To exercise your rights, email SUPPORT_EMAIL.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In the Netherlands this is the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens (autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl).

7. Children

The App collects no data from anyone, including children.

8. Changes

If we change how the App or Website handles data, we will update this policy and the effective date above. If a future version of the App were ever to collect data, we would say so here and in the App Store privacy label before that version is released.
